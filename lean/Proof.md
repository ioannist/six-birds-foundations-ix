## Native model

Use a finite projected carrier B with a uniform stationary law and a family of balanced, pairwise orthogonal sign probes chi on B. The nontrivial characters of a finite elementary abelian binary group supply such probes. Fix rational 0 < kappa < 1. Conditional on mark m, either refresh to a uniform state, or select a probe chi with probability |a_(m,chi)| and sample uniformly from states whose chi-sign agrees or disagrees with the prepared sign according to a_(m,chi). This produces the conditional probabilities

    Q_m(g,h) = (1 + sum_chi a_(m,chi) chi(g)chi(h)) / |B|,
    sum_chi |a_(m,chi)| <= kappa.

The refresh has probability at least 1-kappa. By balance each probe sign occurs on half the carrier, so the selected probe contributes the conditional kernel (1 + sign(a) chi(g)chi(h)) / |B|. Weighting these contributions by |a| and adding the uniform refresh gives the displayed formula. The probe selection is internal to the projected instrument. For the binary group, the probabilities depend only on displacement. Balance gives the row sums, and orthogonality gives both the response to each probe and the multiplication law for word operators on the declared responses. The uniform eigenvalue is 1. Each probe's word eigenvalue is the product of the corresponding coefficients over the word. The one-step probe response recovers every coefficient, including its sign. No probe requires a choice inside a degenerate eigenspace.

Fix native marks with reversal, positive rational probabilities theta_m bounded below by a fixed rational delta > 0, and impose Q_m=Q_bar(m). The marks are i.i.d. in native time. The hidden register lies in a finite R with full-support rational stationary distribution nu. For each mark and ordered base pair g,h the conditional register transition is L_(m,g,h) with stationary margins nu, reciprocal adjoint pairing across (m,g,h) and (bar(m),h,g), and rational entries. The actual marked probability from (g,u) to (h,v) is theta_m Q_m(g,h) L_(m,g,h)(u,v). These allow genuinely coupled base and register dynamics. Since Q_m is uniform-stationary and every conditional L has stationary law nu, the full law is stationary under uniform-times-nu. Reverse support is paired, and the stationary marked edge ratio is theta_m/theta_bar(m), so keeping the marks gives exact path reversal audit as in the accepted proof. The conditional register tables are native preparations checked given the base transition. A physical gate in this hierarchy restricts register proposals conditional on those base outcomes, retaining mark and projected probabilities.

The register disclosure schedule is nested. These are stronger conditional probes than unconditional lumpability and imply it. Equivalently, test just terminal blocks for every conditional table; coarser block masses sum. ## The theorem

For two lawful endpoints, the following are equivalent.

1. At every positive rational tolerance there is a finite factorization by the six effects. Every numerical factor is smaller than that tolerance in all native marked one-step probabilities. All held source tests pass throughout.
2. The profile of the source $A$ precedes or equals the profile of the target $B$, written $p_A\preceq p_B$. In this direction support can shrink, disclosures can refine, and the held license and publication sets can grow. Relative to the initial held tests, the spectra have identical signs on their least reachable supports, with the remaining coordinates set to zero.

The criterion is computed from held exponent rows and sign supports. It does not quantify over physical paths. Each of the six effects is necessary on a single coupled source. The sign obstruction has endpoints at the same profile, so $p_A\preceq p_B$ holds, but their differing surviving signs give a strictly positive obstruction in the native metric.

## The exact spectral criterion

A chart is the signs of all spectral coordinates, including zeros. Fix the old held spectral tests. On a chart S of nonzero indices and its signs, each monomial comparison has either both sides zero (inactive) or both nonzero (active, with compatible signs). For active rows use the exponent difference matrix A_S on S. A contraction S → T keeps signs on T subset S and is permitted if a vector w of nonnegative integers on S is zero exactly on T and A_S w=0. A constant side of a calibration is the monomial with zero exponents. A zero calibration already forces its coordinate out of S. The relation can be decided by rational linear feasibility, testing w_i>=1 on S\T with w_i=0 on T and clearing denominators. This depends on the inherited source equations, with no connectivity question as an input.

Soundness and rational realization. From any rational point a in S, use a_i(t)=a_i t^(w_i) for rational 0<t<=1 and set the positive-weight coordinates to zero at t=0. All live equations hold by their equal total weights, then by the polynomial identity at t=0. Every per-mark absolute sum decreases, hence the uniform-refresh guard holds throughout. The zero limit is rational. Sampling the continuous polynomial curve at a fine rational mesh gives arbitrarily small exact rational steps. This proves that every permitted contraction takes the whole chart into an inhabited chart of the new signs.

Restriction and a unique irreducible chart. A contraction S→T restricts to any U⊆S as U→U∩T, by cutting its weights to U. Rows active in U have zero exponents outside U and were active in S. Signs restrict the same way. This gives a diamond by intersection for any two competing contractions, and applies after rows become inactive. Strict contractions terminate. A stronger direct proof picks a minimum-cardinality descendant C of S. For every descendant U, restrict the path from S to C to U, reaching U∩C. It is a descendant of S, so minimality forces C⊆U. This makes C unique, reachable from each U, and invariant under contraction. Attach the original signs on that support. EqCore below is equality of these signed minimal descendants, not only equal supports. A frequently useful executable version repeatedly zeroes the union of feasible vanishing sets (sum the witnesses), recomputing the active rows until stable.

Every collision of real closures is detected. For real a_n in one fixed sign chart S converging to b, put T=supp(b), Z=S\T. Thus ker A_Z has a strictly positive real vector after negation. A rational basis of the rational homogeneous system and approximation preserve strict positivity, giving integer weights by clearing denominators. Extend by zero on T. The resulting allowed contraction reaches exactly the sign chart of b. No rational lift of this particular b is needed. Within each sign chart, rational points admit arbitrarily fine chains. In log magnitudes, the active constraints are affine. The per-mark absolute-sum bound is a sum of exponentials; its sublevel set is convex. For two rational endpoints the log segment has strict inequalities at interior times for every mark block whose coordinates differ, by strict convexity of exponential on a differing coordinate. ## A control for the physical class

In G=(Z/2)^2 the coefficients (4/5,4/5,7/10) at the two generators and their product give row numerators 33/10,3/10,3/10,1/10, all positive. Holding the first two fixed while damping the third to zero gives a negative row numerator -3/5. Thus merely assuming positive transition probabilities does not justify the contraction realization. The independent parity-mixture budget supplies precisely the coordinatewise downward closure needed in the sufficiency direction.
