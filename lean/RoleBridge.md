## Elementary edits from experiments

Read native support from the positive measured entries. Order reached capability profiles by support contraction, extension of available decisions at every grade, and extension of the license and receipt sets. To recover the observation partition from the available decisions, test the indicator of each partition class. Two preparations agree on every available decision precisely when they lie in the same class. This gives the refinement order at each native grade.

At tolerance ε, an elementary edit either fixes the capabilities and changes each native probability by less than ε, or follows a cover of the reached capability order. A cover with unchanged support retains the prepared law. A strict support contraction retains the projected marked probabilities. These prescriptions refer to the experiments before any effect labels are used. Equality of projected marked probabilities is equivalent to equality of rates and spectra. Sum over the final base coordinate to recover the rate, divide by the positive rate, and take the prepared sign response. Equality of the full prepared law also recovers the conditional register table because the projected marked probabilities are positive.

The cover proof in [Proof.md](Proof.md) applies to this reached order. When support changes, the later conditional register at the narrower support can be combined with the old rates and spectrum, holding every old test. A cover therefore changes support alone. At unchanged support, the later law realizes an advance in licenses or receipts separately from the other capabilities. If only decisions differ, advance the latest differing grade first. Nested disclosure and the inherited conditional mass tests hold at that intermediate profile, so a cover changes only one grade. The native clock distinguishes an earlier decision change from a terminal decision change. Fixed capabilities give the numerical edit type. These arguments give an exclusive classification. Each type has a nonidentity example at every positive tolerance, since deleting its identity steps from a fine factorization of the adjacent witness pair would otherwise contradict the omission bound.

Clock grades enter through observations, not through names for the effect types. The same binary register decision is unavailable at grade 0 immediately before the earlier disclosure and available there immediately after it. Its terminal availability stays fixed. Conversely, in the terminal packaging edit the decision first becomes possible at grade 1 while grade-0 decisions stay fixed. The prepared law is the same in both edits. A translation preserving the named experiments preserves both tests, so it preserves this distinction. ### W₁(a). Descent of the updated native law

The state records are (previous mark, base bit, register cell). Draw a uniform token consisting of a mark, an index in Fin 16 and an index in Fin 4. The output mark is the drawn mark. The base stays put if the first index falls below the count for that mark and flips otherwise. The counts are (10, 10, 7, 9) before the rewrite and (10, 10, 9, 9) after it. The register output equals the second index except at the gated mark and base pair. There, if the prepared register and drawn index both lie in {0,1}, the output is the prepared register. The independent counts out of 4, 16 and 4 draws give exactly the native probabilities of both laws, including the deleted entry.

Record the new fixed-token update for (c, 7, 0), a total self-map on the whole state carrier. Group the source by prepared base and the target by output mark and base. The domain is saturated. States with the same prepared base and distinct register values 0 and 1 give a related distinct pair in the domain. Every related pair has related images, since the output mark and base depend only on the token and the prepared base. These are the three conjuncts of W₁(a), with the total-domain guards. The older law sends the same token and prepared base to a different target class. The two token families return different native c-projections. Every elementary type other than numerical rewrite fixes that measured projection. [TokenDescent.lean](MovingProjection/TokenDescent.lean)

## Return to factorization and the obstruction

The record translation of a state stores the finite graph of the rational prepared-transition table and the finite sets of available decisions, licenses and citeable receipts. For each indexed trial, the graph has exactly one matching entry and reading it recovers the original value. The three finite membership tests recover the other responses. Marginals, word tests and calibrations are then evaluated on the original probabilities. Dividing a prepared signed expectation by its marked mass recovers the spectral coordinate, and the held licenses and receipts recover the exponent rows. The signed core can therefore be computed from the recorded fields.

The finite witness description contains the snapshots used by its witnesses. The class for the edit and obstruction results consists of all attainable query tables. Exact read-back gives an equivalence of states with those tables and preserves and reflects the cover and local relations, including the observable indicating each effect. Lift finite chains along the equivalence in both directions, with or without a specified effect. Thus on the tables, the signed-core criterion characterizes factorization at every positive rational tolerance, the joint edit requires all six effects, and the sign-flip obstruction at 1/272 persists.
